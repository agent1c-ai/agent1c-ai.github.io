Five-minute demo script

What works today

Hackathon-ready, not hand-wavy

The current build is deliberately scoped around a judge-safe demo: visible OS surface, Solana dapp panels, and read-only wallet context.

1

Web desktop

Windows, app launcher, notes, browser, persistent layout, and Hitomi running in the workspace.

2

Solana login path

Wallet detection and Web3 auth path are present in the codebase for Solana identity.

3

Wallet reads

Read-only RPC balance, recent signatures, and parsed transaction summaries are the intended wallet surface.

4

Dapp launcher

Six Solana apps are registered with real icons and open as HedgeyOS app windows.

Read-only demo boundary: Agent1c does not sign, send, approve, swap, custody funds, or access private keys in the current build.
Why it matters

A home surface for serious Solana sessions

Colosseum judges evaluate functionality, impact, novelty, UX, open-source composability, and business plan. Agent1c is designed to speak to all six.

Impact

Solana users do not need another tab. They need a persistent place to run DeFi, DePIN, notes, analytics, and AI context together.

Novelty

The product is positioned as a browser-native operating system for onchain work, not a single dapp or generic assistant.

Composability

The app registry makes more ecosystem products easy to add as panels, with each dapp keeping its own native product surface.

If we win

Token-staked AI compute access

Winning the hackathon unlocks the next public milestone: a Solana-native payment model for Hitomi and Agent1c compute.

Stake Agent1c utility tokens to unlock free AI compute allowance.

Users lock tokens for a chosen period and receive an AI compute allowance while the stake remains locked. The allowance can be spent on Hitomi, tools, and premium workspace actions.

Utility framing: no APY promise, no passive-income claim, no investment-return guarantee. Final token mechanics are subject to legal and compliance review.
